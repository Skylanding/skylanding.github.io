# COLM26 Travel Notes from San Francisco

This year's COLM took place in San Francisco in October. As one of the most well-regarded AI/LLM conferences in North America, it was not one to miss.

![Downtown San Francisco](../img/blog/1015_26/street.jpg)

It coincided with SF Tech Week, so besides the crowds inside the venue, summits and meetups were in full swing all over the city.

![The city at night](../img/blog/1015_26/night.jpg)

At this conference I mostly followed my collaborators around and met a lot of new friends, and I had many meals with fellow Wuhan University alumni. Honestly, I don't think I got to know this many people even during undergrad. Standing by the poster is the excuse; socializing is the real point of going to a conference.

![Pier 39](../img/blog/1015_26/pier39.jpg)

Two dinners also left a strong impression. Meta's was in a small bar, where everyone chatted casually and I made a few new friends.

![Meta dinner](../img/blog/1015_26/meta.jpg)

![The bar](../img/blog/1015_26/meta2.jpg)

Tencent's Qingyun program held theirs at the California Academy of Sciences and sent everyone there by Waymo, which made the whole event feel really thoughtful!

![Invitation](../img/blog/1015_26/invitation.jpg)

![California Academy of Sciences](../img/blog/1015_26/academy.jpg)

We toured the rainforest dome and the aquarium.

![Jellyfish](../img/blog/1015_26/jellyfish.jpg)

![Coral](../img/blog/1015_26/coral.jpg)

At the reception I talked with the head of video algorithms about where the industry is right now, and I met many more new friends.

![Reception](../img/blog/1015_26/drink.jpg)

Most importantly, I won a one-year Tencent Video membership in the raffle! I will be a loyal Tencent fan until ByteDance scoops me up again :D

![Tencent Video membership card](../img/blog/1015_26/raffle.jpg)
